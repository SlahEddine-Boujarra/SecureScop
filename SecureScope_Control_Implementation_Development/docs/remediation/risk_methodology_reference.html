Multi-Dimensional Impact Evaluation

Defined criteria baseline for Level 1 to 5 scoring

tune
1
Negligible Financial Impact

Direct loss < $10,000. Absorbed within standard department operational budget without deviation.

2
Minor Financial Impact

Losses between $10,000 – $100,000. Departmental reallocation required, no external disclosure.

3
Moderate Financial Impact

Losses between $100,000 – $500,000. Executive notice required; material effect on quarterly division EBITDA.

4
Major Financial Impact

Losses between $500,000 – $2,000,000. Insurance policy notification triggered; board review required.

5
Catastrophic Financial Impact

Direct and indirect exposure > $2,000,000. Threatens entity solvency or annual profitability metrics.
